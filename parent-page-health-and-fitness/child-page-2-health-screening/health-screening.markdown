In April 2026 I did a full health screen at a metabolic clinic: a big blood panel, a urine test and a DEXA scan for body composition and bone density. A few things came back off: borderline blood sugar, LDL cholesterol above my doctor's target, low vitamin D and zinc, mild anaemia, and more fat around my middle than I'd like. So I spent the next six months working on it, and retested in October 2026.

This page is the overview: what the reports track, what changed, and what I think drove it. Each marker then gets its own article explaining what it is, what I'm aiming for, why the April number was a concern, and what probably made the October number better (or worse).

## What I changed between April and October

| | What I did |
|---|---|
| **Supplements** | Vitamin D3 5,000 IU every evening (now cut back to 3× a week) and zinc 50 mg a day, both from April |
| **Diet** | More protein, and lower-GI carbs such as brown rice instead of white |
| **Training** | BJJ at New Era MMA, building from once a week to 2–3× a week, then 6× a week in prep for a no-gi competition on 11 October. A full-body strength and conditioning programme from September |
| **Weight** | 70.5 kg in April, cut to 63.6 kg for the 65 kg division, 64.8 kg at the October scan |

Not everything went to plan. I picked up a neck disc injury in early October, and my right elbow got hyperextended by an armbar, first in September and again at the competition. That put me on a course of anti-inflammatories just before the October blood test, and it shows in one result.

## What the reports track

The blood panel and scans cover six areas:

- **Glucose control:** how well my body handles sugar, and how hard insulin has to work.
- **Lipids & heart:** the cholesterol and particle counts that drive heart disease risk, plus inflammation markers.
- **Blood, vitamins & minerals:** red blood cells, vitamin D, zinc, B12 and folate.
- **Kidney & liver:** how well my kidneys filter, and whether my liver is under stress.
- **Body composition (DEXA):** fat, visceral fat, lean mass and bone density, measured by a low-dose X-ray scan.
- **Everything else:** thyroid, electrolytes, hormones and the screening markers that came with the package.

### ↔ Normal both times

Bone density, thyroid (TSH and free T4), electrolytes, calcium, phosphate, magnesium, albumin and platelets were all normal in April and October. Bone density gets its own article, [Bone Density](/health-and-fitness/health-screening/bone-density). The rest, plus the tumour markers, PSA, EBV screen and testosterone that came with the April package, are in [The Other Markers in My Package](/health-and-fitness/health-screening/other-markers).

## The short version

- **Every target my doctor set in April is now met:** LDL, ApoB, vitamin D, visceral fat, fat distribution and insulin resistance.
- **Both deficiencies are fixed** (vitamin D and zinc), and the mild anaemia has mostly resolved.
- **Two things to sort out:** HbA1c crept up even though every other glucose marker improved, and ALT (a liver enzyme) jumped, most likely from the anti-inflammatories I took for my neck. I'm now wearing a glucose sensor to dig into the first; you can follow along on my [glucose readings page](/health-and-fitness/glucose-tracking/). The second gets a repeat liver test in 4–6 weeks.
